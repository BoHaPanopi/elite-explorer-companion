Accordingly:

- the portrait is not represented as a wholly independent original work;
- it is not included in the GPL license for the project's code;
- it must be treated as a separate OGG creative and brand asset;
- its relationship to the project-owner-provided Elite Dangerous Holo-Me reference must remain transparent; and
- this project does not claim rights belonging to Frontier Developments or any other rights holder.

The repository does not currently establish what permissions, if any, would be required for reuse or redistribution of this portrait outside the project. No such permissions are asserted here.
